AutoApply

Your agentic AI job-search assistant: it tailors your resume to every job, applies with your approval, and emails startups for you. Runs on your own computer.

Download AutoApply

Free and open source · macOS and Windows · No Docker needed

Mac: install with one command (no security warning). Open Terminal and paste:

curl -fsSL https://abhi-420bytes.github.io/autoapply-agent/install.sh | bash

Run it again any time to update. Your data is kept.

Tailored resumes

An AI writer and an ATS reviewer agent improve each LaTeX resume, using only facts from your own data.

Applies for you

Reads job emails and job sites, and applies on company portals after your approval. Never bypasses CAPTCHAs.

Startup outreach

Finds startups and their hiring emails, and sends short, specific emails with your tailored resume.

Private

Everything stays on your computer. Keys are encrypted, and any AI provider works (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Ollama…).

Mac, first launch: if macOS says "AutoApply Not Opened", click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway (only once).
Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.
